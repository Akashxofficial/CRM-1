📌 OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
What is this Form and What Makes it Special?
Slide 02 / 10
🎯 What does this form do?

It is the official real estate application used when a customer visits the office, calls, or fills an online inquiry for our luxury project (like AXIO).

1. Captures Verified Information
Gathers Customer Name, Phone, Email, City, Job Designation, and Family details cleanly.
2. Discovers Customer Needs
Finds out if they want a Plot, 2/3 BHK, or Serviced Apartment, their budget (e.g., 50L - 1Cr), and when they plan to buy.
3. Connects Everything in Real Time
Instantly saves the lead to database, sends emails to both customer and manager, and can print an official paper application.
💡 Why is it much better than standard forms?

Traditional forms accept wrong phone numbers, misspelled emails, and require manual data re-entry. This engine solves all 3 problems:

✓ Zero Bad Data
Letters cannot be typed into the phone number box. Dummy numbers like "1234" are immediately stopped.
✓ One-Click Auto Suggestions
Country selection automatically changes the flag and dial code (+91). Email domains auto-suggest on typing `@`.
✓ Dual Purpose: Digital & Paper
Can be filled digitally by an agent, or printed out as a signed paper form for walk-in clients.

🛡️ DATA ACCURACY & VALIDATION
How We Prevent Fake Phone Numbers and Misspelled Emails
Slide 04 / 10
📱 Strict Mobile Number Verification

Prevents agents and users from entering wrong or corrupted contact numbers:

1. No Letters or Symbols Allowed
If someone tries to type "abc" or "!@#", the form instantly discards them. Only digits 0-9 are allowed.
2. Live Counter (e.g., "0/10 Digits")
Displays exactly how many digits have been typed so the user knows when it's complete.
3. Real Indian Telecom Rule Check
For India (+91), the number must start with 6, 7, 8, or 9 (official TRAI telecom series). Starting with 1, 2, or 3 is blocked.
📧 Live Smart Email Domain Suggester

Makes typing emails twice as fast and stops typos like "gmai.con":

1. Typeahead Trigger
As soon as the user enters their name or types @, helpful chips appear below the box.
2. One-Click Popular Domains
Chips for gmail.com, yahoo.com, outlook.com, and urbangaon.com appear.
3. 1-Click Auto-Complete
Clicking a chip instantly completes the email (e.g. "rajesh" + click "@gmail.com" → rajesh@gmail.com).

🔥 INTELLIGENT LEAD SCORING
Automatic Lead Priority (HOT vs WARM) & Channel Attribution
Slide 06 / 10
🔥 Automatic Lead Priority Calculation

The sales team doesn't have to guess which lead to call first. The form categorizes them automatically:

HOT LEAD 🔥 When Buying "Within 1 Month"
Customer wants to finalize urgently. Marked as high-priority so agents call within 15 minutes.
WARM LEAD ⚡ When Buying in "1-3 Months"
Customer is actively exploring. Enters regular nurture and site-visit scheduling workflow.
💰 Budget Converted to Clean Numbers
"₹50L - ₹1 Crore" is automatically converted to min: 50,00,000 and max: 1,00,00,000 in database so searches and filters work smoothly.
🤝 Attribution (Where Did the Lead Come From?)

Tracks where customers originated to calculate channel partner commissions and marketing ROI:

1. Channel Partner (Property Broker)
Selecting "Channel Partner" opens a box to type the broker's agency name. Automatically tagged to that partner.
2. Employee Referral
Selecting "Employee Referral" captures the Staff Employee ID (empId) for internal reward tracking.
3. Digital / Direct / Social
Tags leads arriving from Website, Google Ads, or Social Media campaigns.
